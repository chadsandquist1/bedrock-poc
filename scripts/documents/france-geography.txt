France Geography Overview

France is a country located in Western Europe. It is one of the largest countries in Europe by area and has a diverse landscape including mountains, beaches, and countryside.

The capital of France is Paris. Paris is located in the north-central part of the country along the Seine River. It is the most populous city in France with over 2 million residents in the city proper and over 12 million in the metropolitan area.

Paris is known for many famous landmarks including the Eiffel Tower, the Louvre Museum, Notre-Dame Cathedral, and the Arc de Triomphe. The city has been an important center of finance, diplomacy, commerce, fashion, gastronomy, science, and the arts.

Other major cities in France include Marseille, Lyon, Toulouse, and Nice.
