William Shakespeare: The Bard of Avon

William Shakespeare (1564-1616) was an English playwright, poet, and actor, widely regarded as the greatest writer in the English language and the world's greatest dramatist.

Life and Career:
- Born in Stratford-upon-Avon, England
- Married Anne Hathaway in 1582
- Worked with the Lord Chamberlain's Men acting company
- Part owner of the Globe Theatre in London

Major Works:

Tragedies:
- Hamlet
- Macbeth
- Othello
- King Lear

Comedies:
- A Midsummer Night's Dream
- The Merchant of Venice
- Much Ado About Nothing
- Twelfth Night

Romeo and Juliet:
William Shakespeare wrote Romeo and Juliet around 1594-1596. It is one of his most famous tragedies, telling the story of two young lovers from feuding families in Verona, Italy. The play explores themes of love, fate, and the destructive nature of feuds. It has been adapted countless times for stage, film, and other media.

Shakespeare's influence on literature, theater, and the English language itself is immeasurable. He invented over 1,700 words and phrases that we still use today.
